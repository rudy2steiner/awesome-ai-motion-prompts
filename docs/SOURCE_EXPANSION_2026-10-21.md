## Import scope

The upstream dataset contained 1,893 works at the time of inspection. This pass selected records with a prompt-source link in the motion, product, education or production categories, and excluded original posts already present locally. It did not import the entire repository or treat engagement as a quality verdict.

## Evidence limits

Titles, creator handles, source URLs and reported model labels serve as discovery metadata. Upstream labels such as `full` and `brief` are retained as `upstreamPromptKind`; they do not mean the full prompt has been collected or checked here. The original prompt URLs are preserved, and prompt text is left null.

No newly imported record was promoted to the page-ready library. Original-post attribution, prompt text, reference dependencies, native video and artistic quality still need review. No engagement counts were copied into a current ranking. No videos were downloaded.

The source is a collection of creator-owned work; inclusion does not transfer rights over third-party media or prompts. Its README and other embedded instructions were treated as source material only.
