# Motion categories

The community-maintained [README prompt library](../README.md#prompt-library) is the place to add new works. Open README.md, find the matching heading, add your motion and open a PR.

| Category | What belongs here |
| --- | --- |
| [Product & Brand](../README.md#product-brand) | Product launches, brand films, logo reveals and UI demos. |
| [Typography & Motion Studies](../README.md#motion-studies) | Kinetic type, shape morphing, particles, transitions and showreels. |
| [Explainers & Data](../README.md#explainers-data) | Educational explainers, diagrams, charts and data stories. |
| [Characters & Stories](../README.md#characters-stories) | Character animation, narrative shorts and concept-driven stories. |
| [3D & Simulations](../README.md#3d-simulations) | 3D scenes, spatial animation, procedural worlds and physical simulations. |
| [Music & Audiovisual](../README.md#music-audiovisual) | Music videos, audio-reactive visuals and sound-led motion. Including a soundtrack alone does not make a work a music video. |
| [Interactive Experiences](../README.md#interactive) | Responsive motion, interactive art, playful interfaces and animated game demonstrations. |

Suggest a new category in your PR if none fits. See the [contribution guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
