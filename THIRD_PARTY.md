# Third-party content

Videos, images, prompts, logos, fonts, source projects and skills belong to their respective creators. A public post is a source, not an automatic redistribution license. Follow the original license and obtain permission where needed for reuse.

The initial migration comes from the user's existing `open-prompts` workspace. Original author, work, prompt and discovery URLs are preserved. Preview images retain their source attribution in `data/provenance.json` when available. Full videos are linked rather than stored in this repository.

Do not assume that an upstream repository's software license covers the third-party media or prompts it references. This repository does not grant a new license over those materials.

Send correction or removal requests through an issue identifying the record and original URL.
