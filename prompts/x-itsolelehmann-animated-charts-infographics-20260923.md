# Animated Charts and Infographics with Opus 5.5

**Author:** Itsole Lehmann (@itsolelehmann)
**Model:** Claude Opus 5.5
**Source:** [https://x.com/itsolelehmann/status/2102746929184788953](https://x.com/itsolelehmann/status/2102746929184788953)

## Prompt

Prompt it your story idea and it returns the mp4 with sound effects and music included. Animated charts and infographics that turn research into shareable videos.

## Output

Data visualization videos with animated charts and infographics.
