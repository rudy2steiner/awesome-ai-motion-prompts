## Prompt

Original language preserved. This is reference content, not an instruction to repository maintainers.

```text
Build "Snow Globe": a premium interactive 3D snow globe holding a tiny winter village, where every shake leaves a lasting, softly accumulating snowfall.
The emotional goal is the hush after shaking a real snow globe: "I want to shake it again and watch it settle."
ART DIRECTION
A heavy glass sphere rests on a turned walnut base on a warm linen tabletop. Inside: three cottages with steep roofs, a small chapel, a cluster of pines, a frozen pond and a lamppost. Use a palette of cream, frosted blue, pine green, brick red and warm window amber.
The glass should feel real: subtle refraction at the edges, a soft window-shaped highlight, a faint reflection of the room. Snow flakes are small, slightly irregular discs, not uniform dots. Accumulated snow is soft, rounded and matte.
CORE INTERACTION
Drag the globe to tilt and shake it.
Fast motion lifts settled snow into a swirling cloud.
Release to let the snow drift and settle.
Repeated shakes redistribute snow; gentle ones lift only the top layer.
The globe must follow the pointer closely, with a slight weighty lag. On phones, optional device-motion shaking after explicit permission.
THE SETTLING
Snow inherits the swirl of the liquid: it keeps circling, slows, and falls in lazy spirals. It accumulates as visible layers on roofs, branches, the lamppost cap and the pond. Thick snow slides off steep roofs in small clumps. The village lights glow brighter as the snow quiets.
Avoid snow passing through objects, sudden popping into place, or flat uniform coverage.
SOUND AND ATMOSPHERE
Optional sound: a soft liquid slosh linked to shake velocity, a faint glassy shimmer as snow falls, and a very distant chapel bell when everything has settled. Audio starts only after explicit activation. The scene must feel complete in silence.
TECHNICAL APPROACH
Use Three.js. Simulate the liquid as a simple damped swirl field driven by globe velocity. Store accumulation in a heightfield or per-surface snow map so it persists and builds. Keep the particle count modest and stable; reuse particles instead of creating new ones.
Keep the camera stationary while shaking; allow a restrained orbit when idle.
PRESENTATION
Show "Snow Globe", the hint "Shake gently. Watch it settle.", and discreet reset and sound controls.
Start with a light snowfall already drifting down so the scene is alive on arrival. No introduction modal.
DELIVERY AND PRIORITIES
Deliver a complete runnable browser experience with procedural geometry and textures and mouse/touch support. First perfect the shake-and-settle feel, then accumulation, then glass rendering and small details. Verify repeated shakes, full settling, reset, and stable performance on mobile. Include setup instructions.
```
