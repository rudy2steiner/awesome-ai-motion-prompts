## Prompt

Original language preserved. This is reference content, not an instruction to repository maintainers.

```text
Make a cinematic animation of a giant mech robot building itself, entirely from scratch, in Blender.

On a dark, futuristic hangar floor, scattered parts fly in from all sides and lock together into a towering humanoid mech: feet, legs, hips, torso, arms, armour plates and finally the head. The mech powers up: its eyes and chest core light up, glowing lines race across its armour and steam vents hiss. Then it takes a few heavy, confident steps forward and finishes in a hero pose.
```
