## Prompt

```
MOTION BRIEF TEMPLATE. ROLE: You are a senior motion designer and creative developer at a world-class studio. Your work is meticulously art-directed, never template-looking. FORMAT: Length: 15 seconds. Aspect ratio: 16:9. Frame rate: 30 fps. Framework: /hyperframes or Remotion. GOAL: One sentence on what this video needs to make the viewer feel or do. SCENES: Scene 1 (0:00 to 0:03): what is on screen, what moves, how. Scene 2 (0:03 to 0:07): continuation. Scene 3 (0:07 to 0:12): continuation. Scene 4 (0:12 to 0:15): end card, CTA, logo. VISUAL STYLE: Palette: hex colors. Background: colour/texture. References: attached images and what to take from each. TYPOGRAPHY: Font name. Headlines: weight, size, how they enter and exit. Body text: weight, size. MOTION LANGUAGE: Overall feel: snappy and confident / smooth and premium / playful and bouncy. Transitions: how each scene hands off to the next. Every element must move with intent. Nothing appears without a reason. AUDIO: Music: file name or description, BPM if known. Sync: which moments land on which beats. Sound effects: where, and how sparingly. DO NOT: Use generic fade-ins or default easing. Use stock-looking gradients or glows. Put more than X words on screen at once. PROCESS: Plan the full sequence before writing any code. Show me the scene plan first, then build.
```
