Build this interactive 3D experience as a single self-contained index.html that works offline. Keep the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, procedural geometry, textures, and shaders inline, with no external dependencies or downloaded assets. Use browser-native WebGL with a graceful fallback if unavailable. Support desktop and mobile, keyboard-accessible controls, pause/resume, and reduced motion. Construct an original cinematic impossible observatory inspired by historical fantastical prison etchings and precise astronomical instruments, without copying any source image. Build a cavernous architectural volume from marble, aged brass, carved stone, and deep shadow, populated with recursive staircases, elevated bridges, suspended orreries, giant interlocking gears, archways, and portal-like apertures. Make the world mechanically reconfigure in a continuous staged sequence: stairs fold and rotate, bridges extend, concentric armillary rings turn, counterweights descend, and architectural segments align into a fleeting impossible loop before separating again. Use convincing procedural bevel or edge treatment, ambient occlusion approximations, dust motes, warm practical light against cool moonlight, and a strong deliberate camera composition. Include orbit/zoom, a metamorphosis progress slider, mechanism speed, pause/resume, and click-to-follow portal or camera transitions. The geometry should communicate elegant impossible spatial relationships rather than becoming a random pile of boxes and cylinders.
