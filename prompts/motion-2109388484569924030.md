Build a full-screen "Themed Scene" component (1200×1000) with two variants, Light (white background, black lines) and Dark (black background, white lines). A 120w × 150h × 200d box tilted -22° on X and 38° on Y. Its faces are filled with the variant's background color and outlined, so the box reads as solid.c) Diamond (octahedron wireframe): stage 322×308, perspective 1600px. A 281px object turned -90° on Z. It's made of 12 lines, each 1.5px thick, placed freely and positioned with sideways turns, Z turns and depth offsets so they meet at 6 points. It starts the webcam and Google MediaPipe hand tracking (two hands, GPU with CPU fallback). A mirrored 240×180 webcam preview sits in the bottom-right corner with the hand skeleton drawn on top. The object then rewinds about 450ms so it doesn't jump at the end. • RIGHT hand pinch on an object = rotate it. Hand movement turns it freely (about one full turn per 500px).
