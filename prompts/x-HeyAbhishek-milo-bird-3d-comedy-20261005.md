## Prompt

A 30-second vertical 3D animated comedy featuring Milo, a bird character. Scene: Milo floating under a waterfall, gust of wind drags him to a beach via a leaf on his head. Hungry, he spots a large fruit on a rigid palm tree. Physics rules: palm must not bend or move, all motion requires visible physical causes like gravity or wind. No dialogue, only nonverbal sounds.

## Output

Character-driven comedy with strict physics rules for 3D animation.
