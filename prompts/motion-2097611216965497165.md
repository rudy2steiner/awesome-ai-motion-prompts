## Prompt

Original language preserved. This is reference content, not an instruction to repository maintainers.

```text
Create a 30-second vertical cinematic travel montage titled “24 HOURS SOLO IN BANGKOK.” Follow one young Asian male traveler throughout the day. He wears a black baseball cap, oversized dark T-shirt, blue shorts, white sneakers, and a small crossbody bag. Maintain consistent facial features and clothing.

0–6 seconds: Extreme low-angle fisheye shot from waist level as the traveler walks beneath lush green trees and modern curved architecture. Begin with his body appearing as a bright white cutout silhouette, then reveal him naturally. Slight handheld movement, circular lens edges and strong organic lens distortion. Introduce the title progressively in the center: small white typewriter text “24 HOURS SOLO IN,” followed by large rough red brush lettering “BANGKOK.”

6–9 seconds: Hard cut to a busy Bangkok road beneath elevated railway tracks. Cars, motorcycles and a colorful tuk-tuk move through traffic. The camera rushes forward, then performs a fast sideways whip-pan following the tuk-tuk across a vivid red crossing. Add natural motion blur and energetic speed ramps.

9–11 seconds: Wide fisheye establishing shot of a quiet modern park beneath Bangkok’s elevated transport system. Transition into a horizontal split-screen showing two angles of the traveler entering the park. Cut to an exaggerated ground-level shot as he walks past the lens, surrounded by tall trees and warm sunlight.

11–14 seconds: Low-angle reveal of a giant purple lucky-cat installation outside a shopping complex. Cut to the traveler waiting at a red pedestrian crossing, then tilt upward toward elevated tracks, road signs and a curved glass skyscraper against a clear blue sky.

14–17 seconds: First-person downward view of his white sneakers walking over the red crossing. Seamlessly match-cut the red pavement into deep green grass. Continue with a floating POV walk across the lawn, then transition through moving shadows onto a striped pedestrian crossing.

17–19 seconds: Dramatic close-up of the enormous purple cat sculpture surrounded by hundreds of miniature purple cats. Flash the playful hand-drawn white text “HA?!” near the sculpture. Smash-cut to a symmetrical elevator mirror shot where the traveler films himself with a compact camera beneath sharp geometric ceiling lights.

19–22 seconds: Shift from day to night through a fast lighting transition. Track a glowing tuk-tuk speeding through neon-lit Bangkok streets. Use long exposure light trails, strong lateral motion blur, saturated reds, blues and purples.

22–26 seconds: Rapid nighttime montage: steaming street-food wok, neon market signs, vendor hands preparing noodles, crowded sidewalk, motorbikes flowing past the camera. Use macro close-ups, whip-pans, speed ramps and rhythmic match cuts.

26–30 seconds: End with the traveler standing beside the Chao Phraya River, Bangkok’s illuminated skyline behind him. Slowly orbit around him with a wide fisheye lens. He looks toward the city, then briefly turns toward the camera. Finish on a fast zoom-out with the small centered white text: “ONE CITY. ONE DAY.”

Visual treatment: Sony ZV-E1 travel-film aesthetic, 9:16 vertical, 24fps, 180-degree shutter, ultra-wide fisheye lens, rounded black lens edges, mild film grain, subtle halation, rich green shadows, warm sunlit highlights, punchy contrast, slightly underexposed cinematic color grade. Energetic handheld motion balanced with deliberate composition. Realistic Bangkok locations, authentic traffic and natural crowds. Instrumental electronic travel beat with city ambience, footsteps, traffic, tuk-tuk engine, crosswalk sounds, elevator chime and food sizzling. No dialogue, no subtitles, no logos, no malformed faces, no duplicated pedestrians, no flickering architecture.
```
