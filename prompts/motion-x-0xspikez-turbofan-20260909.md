## Prompt

```
An interactive 3D turbofan you can take apart in the browser, built with Three.js. Suck, squeeze, bang, blow, every stage labelled: the fan pulls in a huge mass of air. Most of it bypasses the core and makes most of the thrust. Compressor stages squeeze the air about 40 times, heating it. Fuel burns hotter than the turbine metal's melting point. The blades survive on cooling air and heat-resistant coatings. Two shafts, one inside the other, spinning at different speeds.
```
