## Prompt

Original language preserved. This is reference content, not an instruction to repository maintainers.

```text
Build a single self-contained HTML file (canvas, no libraries, no network, no audio) for a 1280x720 landscape game, scaled to fit, where the player flies a striped hot air balloon over endless desert mesas at dawn and drops a hanging mail sack onto glowing landing pads atop mesa towers. Controls: hold Space or touch to fire the burner, hold Down or a second touch zone to open the vent, release key or double tap to drop the sack; there is no horizontal thrust, so the only way to steer is to climb or sink into one of 5 stacked wind bands, each with its own speed between -90 and +90 px/s that slowly drifts every 20 seconds. Buoyancy simulation: envelope temperature eases toward the burner target with a 1.8 s time constant, lift is proportional to envelope temperature minus an ambient temperature that falls with altitude, propane burns at 6 percent per second of burner use, and the balloon has real momentum, so good players plan climbs early. Make the wind visible with 600 streak particles advected through the band field plus curl noise turbulence, drawn as fading 8 frame trails, and with cloth flags on every tower that point the local wind. The mail sack hangs on a 12 segment Verlet rope solved with 8 constraint iterations per frame at a fixed 120 Hz substep; it must swing with the balloon's acceleration, never stretch or explode, collide with the mesa terrain polygons, and score only if it comes to rest on a pad (3 rings of accuracy). Procedural world: a seeded generator builds an endless skyline of flat-topped mesas, arches and spires in 3 parallax layers, placing 1 pad tower every 900 to 1400 px so pads always sit within reach of at least 2 wind bands. Camera follows with velocity lookahead and eases its zoom from 1.0 to 0.8 as the balloon climbs.…
```
