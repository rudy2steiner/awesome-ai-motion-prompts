## Prompt

```
Every time a new model comes out, everyone has it draw 'a pelican riding a bicycle' to judge the model's spatial ability, basically using HTML and SVG for animation. I've gotten aesthetic fatigue from watching all of these. I want you to draw the most complex, most detailed, most exquisite 'pelican riding a bicycle' animation video possible. You can use any technology, no rush, you can take a whole day.

Result: A detailed take on the pelican riding a bicycle benchmark: rendered by a GPU raymarcher Claude wrote itself. No models, textures or audio files; 1,140 frames at 40 samples each, score in code.
```
