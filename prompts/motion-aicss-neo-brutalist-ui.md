## Prompt

```
Make a 10-second neo-brutalist product page: a candy-colored landing page for a savings app stamps itself together with thick black borders and hard shadows.

<inputs>
- Product: Jarloo (fictional), a savings app.
- Headline: "Save like you mean it."
- Jars: Holiday $640.00 of $1,200, Bike $310.00 of $450, Rainy day $1,180.00 of $2,000.
- Stickers: NEW! . 0% FEES . * 4.9 . NO SPREADSHEETS
</inputs>

<direction>
- Palette: page oat #F4EFE3 with dotted grid; ink #111111; tomato #FF5A36; butter #FFD84D; pistachio #B6E57A; periwinkle #8EA2FF.
- 4px black borders, hard offset shadows 8px right and 8px down.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 for display; Space Grotesk 500-700 for body; Space Mono 700 for labels.
- Motion verbs: stamp (scale 1.08, 24px above), press (button into shadow), slap (stickers), run (marquees).
Banned: gradients, blurred shadows, glass effects, corner radii over 16px, hairline borders, photos.
</direction>

<build>
One self-contained index.html, 1920x1080 stage, 60fps. HTML/CSS with inline SVG; no canvas and no libraries.
</build>
```
