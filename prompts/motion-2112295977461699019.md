## Prompt

Original language preserved. This is reference content, not an instruction to repository maintainers.

```text
Create one complete, self-contained HTML file for CIDER POND, a bright October afternoon on a small forest lake where a mallard family paddles among falling leaves, as a self-running three.js scene with no interaction.  Use a warm, painterly autumn aesthetic: pale sky-reflecting water as the brightest surface in frame; scarlet and red maples, gold birches and aspens, rust and copper oaks, all deciduous, with not a single pine, conifer or evergreen anywhere; muted olive reeds and lily pads; warm brown trunks and dock timber, never black; a thin matching haze; and a tilt-shift miniature look applied as a post-process pass over the final frame: a strong depth-of-field blur across the top and bottom of the frame with a sharp horizontal band of focus through the pond, and slightly boosted saturation and contrast, so the whole scene reads like a toy diorama. The result should feel like a sunlit storybook painting while still being a smooth, detailed real-time scene. Do not make it dark, crushed, noisy or visibly low-poly, and never a stained-glass look.  Include a large still lake ringed by natural deciduous trees with visible trunks and real branching, each crown an irregular layered mass of clustered autumn colour rather than a single sphere or cone on a stick, varied in height, some half-bare; a mallard hen leading a line of ducklings, several drakes with green heads and white neck rings, and one white farm duck, all leaving V-shaped wakes that spread and fade; ducks that paddle in smooth arcs toward wandering waypoints, tip tail-up to dabble, preen on a rock or the dock, splash gently if they bump, and never leave the water; cattails and reeds along the edges, lily pads in the shallows, a small wooden dock with a footbridge, a mossy log, a short stone path with a bench, and mushrooms at the tree roots; a heron standing in the shallows; dragonflies skimming the reeds; and leaves falling, spiralling down, settling on the water, drifting in rafts and pushed aside as a duck passes. Build every tree, bird and plant from geometry and materials in code; never hotlink a model or an image. Reflect the coloured trees softly on the water, broken by the leaves and the wakes.  Add slow cinematic motion: a high three-quarter camera that starts wide on the whole lake, pushes in and orbits closer over 25 to 30 seconds until the ducks and the near shore fill much more of the frame, then eases back out and repeats from a new angle, never dipping low and never showing the sky; a warm directional sun from the upper left throwing crisp shadows on the water and shore, with a soft ambient floor so no shadow is ever black; and a seamless loop with a little camera drift. Make it fill the window, resize cleanly, hold 60 fps and start with zero console errors. Deliver it as a single self-contained HTML file; any libraries are allowed.
```
