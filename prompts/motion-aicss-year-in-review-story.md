## Prompt

```
Make a 10-second year-in-review animation: phone story cards for a fictional listener's year in music, with big numbers, a top-five list, bold color blocks and a new transition at every cut.

<inputs>
- App and listener: default "Tonebook", Noor, 2026
- Minutes listened: default 48,210 (over 33 days)
- Top artists by plays: default Moth Parade 412, Saltwater Choir 365, Kite Theory 301, Juno and the Lowlands 288, Pale Orchard 240
- Top song: default "Glasshouse Summer" by Moth Parade, 96 plays
- Length: default 10 seconds
</inputs>

<direction>
- Format: a 540x960 story at (690, 60), 36px corners, a 10px bezel, five progress segments on top.
- Cards, two colors each: Pink #FF3D7F, Lime #C9F04A, Cobalt #2B3BFF, Orange #FF7A1A, Plum #2B0F3A.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800; numbers 800 at width 75 in fixed-width digit slots.
- Rhythm: 120 BPM; cuts and entrances land on beats or half beats.
- Transitions, each different: wipe, circle grow, CSS 3D cube turn, stripes slide in.
Banned: gradients, cross-fades, drop shadows, real artists or apps, emoji, confetti.
</direction>

<build>
One self-contained index.html, 1920x1080 stage, 60fps. HTML/CSS with inline SVG and CSS 3D transforms; no canvas or three.js.
</build>
```
