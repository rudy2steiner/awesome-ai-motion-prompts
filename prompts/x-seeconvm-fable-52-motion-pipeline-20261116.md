# Motion Design with Fable 5.2: From One Prompt to Finished Film

**Author:** seeco (@seeconvm)
**Model:** Claude Fable 5.2 + Claude Mythos 5.2 + Sonnet 5.5 + Opus 5.5 + Blender + Remotion + Three.js + GSAP + FFmpeg + Playwright + librosa + faster-whisper
**Source:** [https://x.com/seeconvm/status/2122405510779375668](https://x.com/seeconvm/status/2122405510779375668)

## Prompt

Make a [length]-second animated video as one HTML file. Everything is drawn in code: no images. TIMELINE: [0.0-1.0s]: [what happens]. MOTION: nothing moves in a straight line at constant speed. Entrances ease out, exits ease in. Every spring is a closed-form function of t, damping ratio as written. QA: stills at listed times AND contact sheet every 0.25s. Render: 60fps MP4, frame by frame.

## Output

Comprehensive director's brief template for code-based motion design and music video production.
