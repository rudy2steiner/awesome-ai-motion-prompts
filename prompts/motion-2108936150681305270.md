## Prompt

Original language preserved. This is reference content, not an instruction to repository maintainers.

```text
Build me a single-file interactive HTML artifact: a cozy little world where my AI agents live and work, shown as cute characters.

Make one adorable character for each of my agents/bots, each with its own little house. If I give you a list of my agents and their jobs, use those; if not, create a small friendly team (a scout, a builder, a reviewer, a router, a helper) as placeholders I can rename. Characters are soft, round, big-eyed, each a different color.

Behaviour, animated and alive:
- By DAY the characters walk around and "work", with little speech bubbles showing what each is doing (e.g. "searching...", "building...", "checking...", "routing..."). They path softly, idle-bob, blink.
- A slow DAY to NIGHT cycle: at night the sky turns deep blue, stars come out, the characters walk home, their windows glow warm, a tiny "zzz" floats up. In the morning they wake and come back out.
- Click a character to open a small card: name, role, and what it's doing right now.
- Soft ambient world: gentle colors, little trees, a path, a pond or fountain, soft shadows.

Style: cozy, charming, premium - warm watercolor or soft low-poly, your call. Aim for "this is adorable, I could watch it forever." Make it beautiful, use lots of tokens, iterate until you're genuinely proud. No external assets — draw everything in code (SVG/canvas/CSS). It should just run.

When a character finishes a task, a tiny sparkle or "+1" floats up. Keep it calm and cute, not busy.
```
