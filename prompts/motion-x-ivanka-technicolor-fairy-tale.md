## Prompt

```
Technicolor Fairy-Tale Style. Main prompt: Create a 16:9 character turnaround sheet in the style of a 1950s three-strip Technicolor fantasy film. Two views: front 3/4 and side profile. Character: A glamorous witch heroine in a flowing pink gown with golden embroidery, holding a crystal staff, red hair in loose waves, determined expression. Jack Cardiff cinematography references: rich saturated color, soft backlight halos on hair, deep focus. Lighting: key light upper left, warm fill, hair light visible. Negative prompt: horror monster, demon, evil creature, dark scary mood, modern clothing, photorealistic human face.
```
