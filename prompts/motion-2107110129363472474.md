## Prompt

Original language preserved. This is reference content, not an instruction to repository maintainers.

```text
I want you to create an immersive, artistic, visually stunning interactive experience - not a basic webpage, not a simple CSS layout, and not just text with decorative effects.

The theme is the transformation of humanity from present-day biological existence into a near-future world where human consciousness is transferred into devices, artificial vessels, or digital environments.

IMPORTANT:
This must feel like a visual experience or digital art piece, not a standard website. Avoid generic sections, cards, simple hero layouts, basic gradients, or a “landing page” look. I want something that feels cinematic, atmospheric, emotional, and unforgettable.

Build it as a single self-contained HTML file with all CSS and JavaScript included. The final result should be polished and visually impressive.

Creative direction:
- The experience should show a clear visual progression:
1. Present-day human life
2. Transition / transformation
3. Post-biological / uploaded consciousness future
- The journey should feel seamless, like moving through phases of existence.
- The visuals should communicate emotion, identity, memory, mortality, transcendence, and the blurring of flesh and machine.
- The experience should feel like a fusion of sci-fi cinema, interactive digital art, and a futuristic museum installation.

Visual requirements:
- Full-screen immersive presentation
- Rich atmosphere, lighting, glow, depth, and motion
- Layered scenes with smooth transitions between phases
- Abstract but beautiful symbolism: neural patterns, dissolving bodies, memory fragments, flowing data, holographic structures, particles, light trails, synthetic forms, digital architectures
- Elegant typography used sparingly and meaningfully
- Strong visual storytelling even without much text
- High-end aesthetic: sophisticated, intentional, and artistic

Interaction requirements:
- The user should feel like they are moving through a transformation, not just scrolling a page
- Add meaningful interactivity: scrolling, mouse movement, hover behavior, scene transitions, or subtle input-based reactions
- The transition from one state to another should feel dramatic and emotionally powerful
- Every scene should feel alive and reactive

Tone:
- Poetic
- Haunting
- Futuristic
- Philosophical
- Beautiful but slightly unsettling
- Emotional rather than corporate

Technical guidance:
- Use advanced front-end techniques to make it visually impressive
- If helpful, use canvas, SVG, WebGL, or Three.js-style visual thinking
- Smooth animations are extremely important
- Prioritize visual cohesion and atmosphere over UI complexity
- Keep performance reasonably optimized, but do not sacrifice the experience by making it too plain

What to avoid:
- Generic webpage design
- Boring section-by-section layout
- Random disconnected animations
- Plain CSS-only “pretty” styling without deeper visual direction
- Overly corporate or product-design aesthetics
- Too much text explaining the concept instead of showing it visually

Goal:
I want the final result to feel like a premium interactive short visual journey - something that makes the viewer pause and think, “wow, this actually feels like the passage from human life into digital existence.”

Make it feel like an experience, not a page.
```
