## Prompt

```
Create a 6-shot cinematic animation sequence featuring three creatures exploring a jungle temple: a big shaggy orange horned monster, a blue scaled dragon with wings, and a small tree-creature with vine limbs. Shot 1 (medium): the monster pushes through giant fern leaves into a clearing, the temple ruins visible behind. Shot 2 (close-up): the dragon lands on a stone pillar, scales catching dappled light, and unfurls wings. Shot 3 (wide): all three stand before the temple entrance, tiny against the massive carved stone face. Shot 4 (tracking): they walk through a corridor covered in glowing glyphs, the monster leading. Shot 5 (insert): the tree-creature touches a wall symbol and it illuminates gold. Shot 6 (wide pull-out): the temple entrance glows as they step inside together. Style: Pixar-quality stylized animation, subsurface-scattered fur and skin, volumetric god rays through canopy, moss-covered stone with carved details. Negative prompt: horror monster, demon, evil creature, dark scary mood, gore, blood, weapons.
```
