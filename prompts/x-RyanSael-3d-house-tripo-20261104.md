# Interactive 3D House with AI-Generated Assets via Tripo

**Author:** Ryan Sael (@RyanSael)
**Model:** Claude Opus 5.5 + Tripo AI
**Source:** [https://x.com/RyanSael/status/2117972323399831578](https://x.com/RyanSael/status/2117972323399831578)

## Prompt

Design a small single-storey house on a 21 x 13m plot. Open living room with glass walls and a carport holding one compact vehicle. Garden featuring lavender and agave plants. Inside: sofa, dining set, and potted monstera. Opus created the Three.js scene, then generated an asset list for Tripo to produce 12 detailed 3D models.

## Output

AI architecture design pipeline combining Opus scene generation with Tripo 3D modeling.
