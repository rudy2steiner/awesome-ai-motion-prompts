## Prompt

Original language preserved. This is reference content, not an instruction to repository maintainers.

```text
THE SHOT SEQUENCE, one continuous move of about 30 seconds that then repeats seamlessly (the last frame flows straight into the first):
  1. ESTABLISHING: wide and low, just above the water among the cattails, slowly dollying forward through a gap in the reeds as the
  afterglow fills the sky and the first V of geese appears small over the far tree line.
  2. THE APPROACH: a slow crane up and a smooth pan as the lens lengthens, picking up the V in silhouette against the brightest part of the
  sky and tracking it as it comes closer and the birds begin to break formation.
  3. THE LANDING: the camera eases back down to just above the water at a normal lens, side-on to the geese as they cup their wings, drop
  their feet and skid onto the water in a spray, their wakes spreading toward the lens with the reflections of the sky rippling.
  4. THE SETTLE: a slow pull back and rise to a wide view of the whole marsh, the landed geese small on the glowing water, the next skein
  appearing far off, and the camera drifting back into the cattails to the first view as the loop closes.
  As the shots play, the light very slowly deepens toward twilight and then gently returns, so the loop restarts without a jump. Add a
  gentle synthesized ambience with Web Audio if you like (soft wind through the reeds, distant goose calls, a splash as they land); if so,
  show a small, unobtrusive "click for sound" hint and never block the scene behind it.
```
