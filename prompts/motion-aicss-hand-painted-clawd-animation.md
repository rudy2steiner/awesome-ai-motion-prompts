# Hand-Painted Cartoon Starter

**Author:** John Heibel (@JohnHeibel)  
**Model:** Claude Opus 5.5  
**Source:** [https://www.aicssanimations.com/directory/Motion-Prompts/hand-painted-clawd-animation](https://www.aicssanimations.com/directory/Motion-Prompts/hand-painted-clawd-animation)  
**License:** CC0

## Prompt

```
A starter kit for hand-painted cartoon films in p5.js and p5.brush: watercolour washes, ink outlines and boiling linework, the Clawd character with 31 emotions, and a storyboard-first contact-sheet loop.

1. The medium is solid: brush strokes, flat 2D, boil
- Paint everything with p5.brush through paint() and inkLine(). Characters get flat wash colour plus an ink outline.
- The linework boils. jit() and random() are reseeded 12 times a second (BOIL), so every drawing wobbles slightly, like hand-drawn animation.
- Everything is flat 2D. Never project 3D.

2. No text
- Show it, don't write it. No captions, no titles, no labels on objects, no speech bubbles with words.
- Clawd's reactions are painted marks, never letters: !, ?, zzz, sweat, hearts, a bulb, a rain cloud.
```
