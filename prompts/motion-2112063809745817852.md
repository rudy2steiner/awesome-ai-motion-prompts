THE CLONE CHAOS 🫧✨

15-Second AI Video Prompt | 9:16 Vertical
SCENE:
A peaceful magical room becomes an adorable, hilarious clone disaster when a tiny fluffy creature accidentally creates miniature copies of itself. Smooth, continuous action with clear progression.

CHARACTER — STRICT IDENTITY LOCK:
Use the provided reference image as the exact character design. Preserve its round cream face, enormous glossy black eyes, rosy cheeks, tiny smile, pink-purple holographic fur with iridescent cyan highlights, two small crystal-like ears, and tiny rounded limbs. Every clone must be an identical miniature copy with matching facial features, colors, fur, and proportions. Never redesign the character.
ENVIRONMENT:
A dreamy pastel mint-green room with a luminous floor, a large magical mirror with an iridescent frame, floating sparkles, and subtle reflections. Keep the background clean and uncluttered.

ACTION & TIMELINE:
0–2s — Curious Reflection:
The creature tilts its head at the mirror, raises a tiny paw, and touches the glass. A glowing cyan ripple spreads across the surface.

2–4s — First Clone:
The reflection pushes through the mirror like magical liquid, becoming a tiny identical creature. It lands beside the original in pastel sparkles. Both stare at each other in surprise.
4–6s — Clone Explosion:
The two creatures touch paws. A colorful magical pulse releases pink, purple, and cyan glitter, creating several identical miniature clones around them.

6–8s — Magical Sneeze:
The original's nose twitches. It closes its eyes and sneezes adorably, releasing glittering pastel dust that creates more clones. The original looks around in confusion.

8–10s — Total Chaos:
Around 8–12 identical mini-clones bounce, waddle, spin, and chase one another. Some peek from behind the original while others hop in circles. The original looks hilariously overwhelmed.
10–12s — Tiny Head Passenger:
One clone jumps onto the original's head and settles between its crystal-like ears. The original freezes and looks upward with a comically confused expression. Nearby clones imitate it.

12–14s — Group Pose:
The original looks at the camera and winks. The clones gather in a cheerful semicircle; a few float inside tiny translucent bubbles as glowing hearts and sparkles swirl around them.
14–15s — Final Moment:
A medium close-up shows the original smiling and winking at the camera, with mini-clones peeking around its face and shoulders. One remains perched on its head as pastel bubbles drift past. End on a polished, adorable frame.

CAMERA ANGLE:
Eye-level medium-wide opening, moving closer as the first clone emerges. Keep the original centered and visible during the chaos. Finish with a centered medium close-up.
CAMERA MOVEMENT:
Smooth dolly-in, gentle lateral tracking, subtle floating motion, and a controlled final push-in. No abrupt cuts or excessive camera shake.

LENS:
35mm-equivalent for opening and group action, transitioning to a 50mm-equivalent close-up. Soft depth of field while keeping nearby clones recognizable.
LIGHTING:
Soft diffused lighting, mint ambient glow, cyan rim lighting, pink and lavender reflections on holographic fur, and soft contact shadows. Magical particles glow gently without overexposure.

COLOR PALETTE:
Pastel mint, cream, holographic pink, lavender, iridescent cyan, and soft white.
EMOTION:
Curiosity, wonder, surprise, playful confusion, joyful chaos, and heartwarming cuteness. Use expressive eyes, subtle head tilts, and natural gestures.

VISUAL DETAILS:
Soft individual fur strands, glossy eyes, iridescent highlights, translucent bubbles, delicate particles, and believable bouncing and landing. Keep clones separated and consistent. Limit the peak to 8–12 clones.
STYLE:
Polished high-quality 3D fantasy animation, soft tactile fur, expressive cute characters, cinematic composition, smooth natural motion, gentle pastel colors, professional social-media animation, 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds.

AUDIO & SOUND DESIGN:
Whimsical instrumental music with soft bells, plucky marimba, and magical synths. Add glass chimes when the mirror activates, sparkling whooshes for clone appearances, soft boing sounds for bouncing, a cute sneeze at 6–8 seconds, and a final twinkle on the wink. Synchronize sounds with actions. No dialogue, narration, or lyrics.
NEGATIVE PROMPT:
No character redesign, different face, altered eyes, extra or missing ears, color changes, humans, realistic animals, scary expressions, distorted anatomy, extra limbs, deformed paws, melted fur, inconsistent clones, sudden size changes, flickering, unwanted morphing, disappearing main character, excessive particles, chaotic camera shake, harsh lighting, photorealism, text, subtitles, logos, watermark, or abrupt ending.

FINAL PRIORITY:
