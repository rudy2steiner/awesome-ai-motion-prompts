## Prompt

Original language preserved. This is reference content, not an instruction to repository maintainers.

```text
Create a 5 sec motion graphics animation of a simple dot, bouncing into the center of the screen, it expands to a search bar and then text fills the box: "I'm a motion designer, what should my next career be?"  The animation should be be fluid, ease keyframes in and out, transition the search bar into a full white frame. a matte will open inside to the start of the animation and create a loop
```
